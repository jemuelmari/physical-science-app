# Physical Science Online Modular App

A GitHub-hosted, offline-capable web application for the DepEd Senior High School
**Physical Science** core subject (Grade 11/12), based on the K to 12 Basic
Education Curriculum (August 2016).

The app presents the curriculum as a **chronological journey through time** —
from the birth of the universe, through Ancient Greek philosophy and alchemy,
to modern atomic theory, chemistry, and Newtonian physics — across **10 weeks
of instruction (4 days/week, 1 hour/lesson)**.

## 🕰️ Chronological Flow

| Week | Era | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | 🌌 Cosmic Origins | Big Bang, stellar nucleosynthesis |
| 2 | 🏛️ Ancient Greece | Greek atoms, elements, spherical Earth |
| 3 | ⚗️ Alchemy → Chemistry | Alchemists, early lab techniques |
| 4 | ⚛️ Atomic Theory | Dalton, discovery of elements |
| 5 | 🔬 Subatomic Revolution | Thomson, Rutherford, Moseley, Bohr |
| 6 | ☢️ Nuclear Age | Atomic number, transmutation |
| 7 | 🧪 Chemical Bonding Era | Polarity, intermolecular forces |
| 8 | 🏭 Industrial Chemistry | Reaction rates, stoichiometry, energy |
| 9 | 🧴 Consumer Chemistry | Cleaning agents, cosmetics |
| 10 | 🔭 Cosmos & Motion | Kepler, Galileo, Newton |

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **10-week modular curriculum** with 40 lessons (1 per day, 4 days/week)
- 📝 **Assessment suite**: 3 quizzes, 2 summative tests, 1 term exam, 1 performance task
- 🔒 **Activity gating** — students unlock assessments after completing lessons
- 📊 **Teacher dashboard** — class record, item analysis, intervention tracker
- 📶 **Offline-first** — works with `localStorage` even without internet
- 🔄 **Optional cloud sync** — Google Apps Script backend writes to a Google Sheet
- 🎯 **Transmutation table** — DepEd-compliant grade conversion
- 📱 **Mobile-responsive** — designed for both desktop and phone
- 🌐 **GitHub Pages** ready — no server needed
