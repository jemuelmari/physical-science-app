[1.0.0] — 2026-01-15
Initial release.

✨ Features
• 10-week chronological curriculum (Cosmic Origins → Cosmos & Motion)
• 40 lessons (4 days/week × 10 weeks × 1 hour/day)
• Full assessment suite:

3 quizzes (physci-quiz1, quiz2, quiz3)

2 summative tests (physci-st1, physci-st2)

1 term exam (physci-te)

1 performance task (physci-pt1)
• 130 total assessment items, all competency-tagged
• Offline-first storage (localStorage)
• Optional Google Apps Script cloud sync
• Teacher dashboard (class record, item analysis, intervention)
• Transmutation table (DepEd-compliant)
• Activity gating (lessons unlock assessments)
• Mobile-responsive design
• GitHub Pages ready

📚 Curriculum Alignment
• DepEd K to 12 Physical Science CG (August 2016)
• All 44 competencies mapped (S11/12PS-… codes)
• Content standards:

Ancient Greek to the present (atomic theory)

Properties of matter and chemical structure

Chemical changes

Household & personal care products

Earth's place in the universe

Universality of physics laws
